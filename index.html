The maker

Old things still have something to say.

I’m Paul Ledger. I make sculpture, furniture and smaller objects in north-west Tasmania, working with reclaimed steel, old mechanical parts and timber that catches my eye.

A worn spanner, a piece of machinery or an old fence post can suggest the shape of the next piece. I enjoy keeping something of that earlier life visible in the finished work.

Paul Ledger · Redwel Designs
